About me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate working on Control of Partial Differential Equations, with a focus on the stabilization of Weakly Hyperbolic Systems of Conservation Laws, under the supervision of Vincent Andrieu, Mathieu Bajodek and Claire Valentin.

Currently in the final year of my Ph.D. I am seeking a postdoctoral position starting in Late 2026 in the fields of Control theory for PDEs, Hyperbolic systems or Numerical analysis.

I'm a member of the team Dynamic, Control and Observation of Process Systems (DYCOP) at LAGEPP UMR CNRS 5007 - Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1.
I'm also a teaching assistant at the Departement of Electrical and Process Engineering.

Teaching

Below are some of the courses I have taught or developed during my PhD.

  • Numerical Approximation of PDEs – 2nd Year Graduate Students in Control Engineering
    Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 • 2023–2026
    Following an opportunity given by Pr. B. Maschke, I designed the final part of the Distributed Parameter Systems course to introduce students to the numerical approximation of infinite-dimensional systems. It covers the construction of Finite Difference schemes for the heat and advection-diffusion equations, as well as Finite Volume methods for hyperbolic systems of conservation laws (1D Saint-Venant equations). I specifically present Godunov schemes and Approximate Riemann solvers, supplemented by the development of computational codes.
    Problem sets: 1 (Heat Equation) / 2 (Advection-Diffusion) / 3 (Saint-Venant)
  • Numerical Methods for Engineers – 1st Year Graduate Students in Control engineering
    Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 • 2024–2026
    Direct and Iterative methods for Sparse Linear Systems and Numerical Optimization (Gradient descent, Conjugate gradient methods, and least-squares problems)
  • Advanced Programming: C Language – 3rd Year Undergraduate Students in Electrical Engineering
    Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 • 2024–2026
    Pointers and arrays, Memory allocation and Dynamic Storage, Structures, Modular programming (sources, headers, makefiles), Low level programming: Bitwise operators, Bitmasking, Bit-fields in structures
  • Introduction to Automatic Control – 2nd Year Undergraduate Students in Electrial Engineering
    Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 • 2024–2026
    Laplace Transform and Convolution, Stability analysis, Frequency domain, Feedback control loops, Elementary controllers (P, PI, PID).
